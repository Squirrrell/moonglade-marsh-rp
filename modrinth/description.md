# Custom Moonglade Marsh

A configurable recolour pack for **Moonglade Marsh** (Galatea) in Hypixel SkyBlock. Recolour the fig and mangrove trees, swap their wood, change the marsh grass, and hide clutter while you forage.

**Requires [Catharsis](https://modrinth.com/mod/catharsis) 1.0.0-beta.22 or newer.**

## Features
- **Fig trees:** choose from 11 wood types and 14 leaf colours.
- **Mangrove trees:** choose from 11 wood types and 14 leaf colours.
- **Grass colour:** grass blocks, short and tall grass, ferns, vines and lily pads.
- **Hide clutter:** hide grass and ferns, or hide lily pads.
- **Mud texture:** packed mud, mud bricks, dirt, coarse dirt, moss or rooted dirt.

Colours: Red, Orange, Yellow, Lime, Green, Swamp Teal, Light Blue, Blue, Pink, Purple, White, Grey, Black, or the default look.

## Galatea only
All changes are limited to Moonglade Marsh, and other islands look exactly as they normally do. The pack works alongside other Catharsis packs, such as island packs for Torrhus Canyon.

## How to use
1. Install Catharsis and enable this pack.
2. Run `/catharsis config moonglade_marsh` in-game.
3. Pick your options. The pack reloads by itself.

## Notes
- Hidden lily pads, grass and ferns still have their hitboxes.
- If any leaves keep their vanilla colour, open an issue with the block name shown on F3.
