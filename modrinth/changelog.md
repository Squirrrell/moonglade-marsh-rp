## 1.2.0

### New
- **Path Color:** recolour the dirt paths, the starting-area sand, sandstone floor tiles and steps, and the oak slab and stair walkways. Sandstone in building walls stays cream.
- **Vegetation Follows Grass Color** (on by default): moss, cave vines, azalea bushes, dripleaf, seagrass, kelp, sugar cane, mangrove propagules, sea pickles, berry bushes, the olive ground blocks and hedge leaves now use your grass colour too, so the whole marsh matches. Fig and mangrove canopies keep their own leaf colours.
- **Water Color:** recolour the water on Moonglade Marsh.
- Grass, path, vegetation and water options are now together in one **Grass & Paths** section.

### Previously in 1.1.0
- **Time of Day:** lock the sky to Sunrise, Day, Sunset or Night, on Moonglade Marsh only or across all of SkyBlock. Only your client sees this; the server time is unchanged.
- Fixed leaves at the edges of fig and mangrove trees keeping their vanilla colour.

**Requires Catharsis 1.0.0-beta.22 or newer.** Open the options with `/catharsis config moonglade_marsh`.
